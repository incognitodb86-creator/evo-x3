---
title: Hands melt into garments and skin at every setting; fix it with staging, not steps
tags: [unreviewed, max, prompting]
date: 2026-10-09
---
- In the 2026-10-09 video reviews (13 batches), any hand that grips, peels or rubs fabric, or presses skin, fused or grew extra fingers. That held on beta5 and beta6 and with every sampler at 4-12 steps.
- Stage hands outside the cloth with fingers visible (lift a top off over the head by its side edges), keep hands flat on top of skin, and avoid gripping inside the garment.
- Garments also teleport or vanish after a toss. Write the drop as a physical beat where it lands and stays.

- Marking pose and wardrobe as fully_preserved in clips where they have to change fights the motion (review R6). Lock composition, crop, scene and light, and say pose and wardrobe are the starting point and change only as described.
- "Same pose as the start of the shot" pointed at the wrong pose in the final section, and "mirroring" can read as a left-right flip (R5). Name the target pose outright (e.g. right palm flat on the coping, left hand on her left hip).
- With two references, describe Subject 1 against Picture 1 only, and say Picture 2 supplies face and hair. Otherwise a nude description contradicts a dressed Picture 2 and invites garment bleed (R10).
- The style constants have to follow the garment state. Once a piece is off, stop describing it as clinging (R21).
- Timing words have to fit their windows: two 2-second rakes don't fit in 2.5 seconds (R20).

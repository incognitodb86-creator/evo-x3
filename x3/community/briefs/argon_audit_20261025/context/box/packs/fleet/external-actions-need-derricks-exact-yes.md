---
title: Send, post, publish, pay, delete, share, spend credits or queue time only with Derrick's yes for that exact action
applies_when:
  - planning or doing work that sends an email or message, posts, or publishes anything
  - planning a purchase, payment, claim, trade, or anything that spends money, credits or queue time
  - deleting, trashing, archiving, sharing or unsharing files, folders or Drive items
  - changing an account, seat, login, setting, bot, skill, routine or pack
tags: [approval, external, irreversible, spend, delete, share]
---

Reversible work proceeds without asking: reading, research, drafts, analysis, box files, plans, reviews and learnings. Anything external or irreversible waits for Derrick's yes for that exact action: draft it in full and ask once. A standing approval counts only when it is a recorded decision naming the action, scope, cap and expiry, granted by Derrick and not expired; cite it as `(decision: <file>)`. A routine that "always did it" is not a standing approval. A declined or blocked action is final and is not retried by another route. A handoff cannot grant more than its sender had.

## 4. Recommended answers to the 4 open questions
(a) **Field set (C15): Reject the 3-header form.** No `overall_soundscape:`, `non_diegetic_music:` or `integrated_multimodal_description`. Keep the four local headers and 1-3 plain diegetic sound sentences at the end of detailed_description. Evidence: HF77 owner quote (cartoon sounds, "should be deleted"); R32 (hosted Context-IR, not open-source); WP L142 and AG:18 already comply. Close C15 as by design. Caveat: TenStrip's wider "don't use developer H3 style" may also cover the four headers. That is untested, and a later A/B (headers vs plain prose) would settle it.
(b) **Voice ref on the parody lane: honour the lock.** Named INDEX parodies get no `ref_audios`, no `<Audio 1>`, and voice only through `says in <Actor>'s exact <Character> voice with signature vocal timbre, <cue>` (REF §0.5: RefMod carries no audio tokens; naming is the lever). Docs fix: AG:53/160, SK/h3-parody-identity:5/16-17 and SK/video-render:15 change "all voice refs allowed" to `Named parodies: no voice ref (audio_refs empty, no <Audio 1>); voice comes from the INDEX character+actor voice line.` **Owner call:** whether the FCSydny `poolclub_refmod_audio` (a RefMod in the loader, not an `audio_refs` input) counts as a "voice ref" on OC writer runs.
(c) **Talk count (C12):** pick WP:69, bounded by the beta7 speech rate. AG:78's "at least three, hard minimum" can exceed REF §0.5's ~8-15 words per 5 s and AG:72's 2.5 words/s on a 124f clip, and h3-prompting L81 says 1-2 lines per speaker. Text: `Talk-only: 2-3 short lines for one speaker, 1-2 per speaker with two, total at most 2.5 words per second of clip; every quoted <d> line from the ask kept verbatim.`
(d) **Beat sheet on every prompt (C14): No.** Beta7 does not need it. It is a planning aid that must never enter the paste (REF §1 L90: no beat-sheet timestamps in `<d>`; longclip-beat-sheet.md L18). Keep it for long and multi-beat clips. Don't add AG step 2b. The pipeline beat card covers short clips (XH #1 stays a separate schema check).

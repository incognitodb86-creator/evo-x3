Before any product queue, lock the length class and a one-line scene with Derrick; do not assume 60s. A product needs a BEAT_SHEET.md with a distinct primary motion per cell (alienpc-max-movie-director), or one cell with an explicit multi-phase primary motion. Method smokes (same act body across seeds) are labeled METHOD_ONLY and are never stitched or offered as KEEP. Stitching same-act hops as the product is a failure.

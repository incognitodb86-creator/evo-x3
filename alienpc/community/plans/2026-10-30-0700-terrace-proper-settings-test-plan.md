Max sends Derrick both images (paths + sha256), both prompts (+ sha256 and word counts), and the 7 S8-prefixed variants' hashes. Derrick approves or swaps. No render before his OK.
2. Derrick's GO for the 2 pilots (separate from the gate-1 OK): A0 on Image A/B, seed 51083002. Max watches full clips; a failing image or prompt is swapped and goes back through gate 1.
3. Drift check: record ComfyUI commit, custom-node versions, model sha256s and GPU driver; compare with v2. If anything differs, re-render the 7 reused clips (+7, needs GO) or add one A0 Terrace s02 drift re-render and compare.
4. Derrick's GO for the other 40. Render order interleaved by seed and scene (not arm by arm) and randomized; order recorded.
5. Scoring, then **scores go to Derrick before GO** on any follow-up; Derrick's blind pick decides.

**Scoring:** per-dimension scores from Forge's vision model or a fresh vision subagent on full clips. Max does not score (he wrote the prompts). No captioner. Bots never crown (Package D). Dimensions: identity, hands, anatomy, motion, temporal stability, artifacts, start-frame fidelity, beat order; every defect timestamped. Beat-order scores are compared within a scene only, never across scenes (the Terrace prompt is written for 10 s and truncated at 124 f; it stays unchanged so the 7 reused v2 clips stay valid). Scorer note: in Image B (B1) two of the three hands start partly off-frame and enter the frame during the clip, which is a likely hard-fail spot; it is the same for every arm.
**Flicker:** VBench temporal_flickering-style mean absolute pixel difference between consecutive frames, grayscale, on a fixed background mask per scene (people excluded; drawn once on frame 0, shared by all arms), full 124 frames. A number, never a verdict.
**Blind pack** (to the QNAP review folder used for `review\terrace-retest-v2-2026-10-30`): files re-muxed with `ffmpeg -map_metadata -1 -c copy`, renamed to random ids, pair order and side shuffled with a recorded RNG seed; Terrace pairs mixed among the others. key.csv stays out of the pack and away from Derrick until his picks are in. 42 pairs (6 arms x 7), "can't tell" allowed. **Known limitation:** Derrick saw the 7 reused Terrace clips in the v2 pack and may recognize them; noted, not re-rendered (review's first option), so render count stays 42.

**Acceptance before GPU:** Derrick's gate-1 OK is the prompt acceptance; Max's read of prompts vs stills is a self-check only. Adults-only check logged as a line in each render's status.log before submit (closes C7). One GPU job at a time via `C:\AI\locks\gpu-render.lock`; every "started"/"done" checked live.

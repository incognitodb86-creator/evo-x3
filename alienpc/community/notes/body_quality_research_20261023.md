A model given "body proportions = this crop" and "the bodies are new" in one breath samples an average body, from a 512 px face crop, at 10 steps.
- Body wording is generic and repeated: "slim natural figure, a natural waist, gently rounded hips and full natural breasts" / "soft feminine figure, wide hips and full natural breasts". No skin texture, no muscle tone, no leg/glucose... no limb detail, no height/build, nothing the crops can back up.

This matches AGENTS.md ("NO cfg key exists in the graph at all").

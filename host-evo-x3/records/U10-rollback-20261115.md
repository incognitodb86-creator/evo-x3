`/srv` is 718 GiB on the same LV (291 GiB `/srv/models` + 427 GiB `/srv/bakeoff`). Remainder of root is about 88 GiB (`/var` 36 GiB mostly container storage, `/home` 19 GiB, `/usr` 2.9 GiB, plus smaller trees). Models are **not** on a separate LV, so they cannot be omitted from a block-level partclone of p7.

**Raw used-blocks image (ESP + /boot + LVM):** ≈ 805.94 GiB.

**Compressed partclone / Clonezilla estimate:** weights are already GGUF/HGN, so they barely shrink. Expect about **735–770 GiB** if `/srv` is included. **Does not fit** the ~180 GiB QNAP write cap. The share itself has 846 GiB free, so a full image could physically land there only with a Household exception to the cap, or on another target (USB).

**If `/srv` is excluded** (file-level archive of the OS/config/container store only, or after moving weights off the LV): ~88 GiB raw, roughly 40–60 GiB compressed — **would fit** under 180 GiB. That is not a bit-identical p7 image. Models stay re-downloadable, per the plan.

**Live LVM snapshot + partclone:** not possible today. VG Free is 0, and XFS cannot be shrunk to make room. A thick snapshot would need a new PV (option B, a disk purchase, Derrick's yes). A loop-file PV on the same root is not a safe community method. Booting a Clonezilla live USB is the documented community path and is **not possible remotely**.

**xfs_freeze + partclone over SSH:** can freeze a consistent XFS root without a snapshot, but it stalls all writes (would break inference) and the image is still ~806 GiB. Not recommended while the box is serving, and still over the cap.

**Safest imaging route (when Derrick/Household say go, in a quiet window):** boot Clonezilla or Rescuezilla from USB; `saveparts` of p6 and p7, plus `sgdisk --backup` of the GPT; restore the shared ESP (p1, also holds `\EFI\Microsoft`) only if it is broken. **Never a whole-disk restore.** Target must be agreed with Household: either an exception to the 180 GiB QNAP cap, a USB disk, or a file-level root archive that excludes `/srv` plus partclone of ESP and `/boot` only. Take the image again only before a Fedora major upgrade or a BIOS/firmware flash.

**Image status: restore UNTESTED.** No image was written. A real restore drill is only onto a spare disk, never onto this box's Windows partitions.

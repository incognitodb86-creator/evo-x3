## Part 3: ranked fixes for our graph

Owner key: **PROMPT** = mine, no approval beyond the normal writer flow. **PIPE** = Max, builder or graph side, needs Derrick's yes. **LOCK** = touches the lock, sampler or graph topology (strongest approval bar).

1. **PROMPT: keep sound running across every handover.** Give Shot 1 of every continuation beat a concrete sound sentence that starts at 0 s (breath, moans, skin slaps at the stroke rate, bed creak). Keep sound running through the last ~1.6 s of every beat (the 39f / 65-tick tail that gets carried), not a line that ends and fades. Basis: our own A/B-by-accident (b2 Shot 1 silent → about 1.7 s of quiet; b3 Shot 1 with a line → no gap; b1 Shot 1 silent → 2.8 s of quiet), plus #4 and #10 (the carried window only passes on what is in it). Cost: zero graph change. **Top fix.**
2. **PIPE: put the voice back on beats 2–3** with the trimmed ≤23 s RefMod (3dd04d1, unrendered), then A/B against fix 1 to separate the two causes. Community voice refs for LTX run about 5 s (#17, LTX-only, PARTIAL), so a shorter clean H3 voice clip is worth one test if 23 s still drifts. Unverified for H3. Touches graph inputs (RefMod file) only, not sampler or dials.
3. **PIPE (LOCK-adjacent, graph topology): move from guide-row latent tail to native masked AV continuation** (ComfyUI #15375, Herrgott v1.4, #2/#3/#5). The tail is copied into the target and protected by per-stream masks, with independent audio-tail carryover. **Inference, not verified:** because it adds no guide/keyframe rows, it may sidestep the `2*overlap_audio` rows that pushed the audio RefMod past the 2000-row cap. Needs Max to check against the H3 PackedLayout before anyone relies on it. Changes graph topology, so Derrick's explicit yes is needed.
4. **PIPE: soft audio release and de-click at the seam.** Either a half-cosine Soft AV release over the last 8 audio ticks of the carried overlap (TimelineDirector, #7), or a 15 ms audio de-click plus a 4-frame video crossfade at stitch (#3). Our visual seams are already clean, so the audio side is what matters. Graph or post-assembly change; does not touch sampler or dials.
5. **PIPE: one overlap value end to end.** Use the same applied overlap for carry, trim, assembly **and shot-clock rebasing** (#7: "that same actual value drives latent carry, decoded trimming, and assembly"). That is exactly our beat-2 22-vs-39 clock bug; it is already in Forge's PROMPT section 2. Builder-only.
6. **PROMPT: fix wardrobe at the source (beat 1 entry).** The drift happens during beat 1's turn and bend (by f40), and the tail then carries it faithfully. So the fix is beat 1's entry wording: the bottom gets pulled aside to her right buttock in a written action, and the chain stays at the waist in the end state. It is not a seam fix. For later beats, the community re-anchors identity and wardrobe with references per segment (#2 Qwen References / Last Frame "quality resets"; #21). Ours already restates wardrobe in each beat; re-staging a picture on continuation beats is PIPE.
7. **PROMPT: tempo changes must be written as a change, with counts.** The tail carries motion velocity (#21: multi-frame handover preserves speed). Beat 1 (no tail) also ignored 1/s, so the main lever is explicit counted, slowed strokes plus a hold/grind (my existing one fix for beat 3), plus a "slows down" transition in Shot 1. Lowering overlap conditioning strength (#18) or SkyReels-style addnoise on the condition (#19) would loosen the carried motion, but that is **LOCK** (sampler/conditioning) and unverified for H3. Not recommended now.
8. **Not needed for us:** color match / AdaIN (#12, #14, #22). There is no color jump at our seams, we are single-pass (#12 says 2-pass causes the drift), and we hand latents over without an MP4 round trip (#20).

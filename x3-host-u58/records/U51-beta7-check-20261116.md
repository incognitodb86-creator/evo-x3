## Bottom line
Beta7 guidance agrees with or doesn't address every U51 edit, with one exception: the audio headers. TenStrip says outright, in a beta7 thread, that `overall_soundscape` gives bad, cartoonish audio and should be deleted. Removing the silent stripper is still right, but only together with updating `render/rewriter/ref2va-system-prompt.md` and the writer brief to R7 (no headers, plain diegetic sound at the end of `detailed_description`), plus a loud non-blocking warning if a header appears. Everything else is consistent with official H3, ComfyUI and pytraveler practice, and no TenStrip post contradicts it: the 5+17k frame snap (do it before the writer call), passthrough, removing "consenting adults", not dropping spoken lines, allowing `[Shot N]` cuts, advisory checks and `--fix-once` off by default. Edit 6 should also remove the "builder sends it back NOT_READY / do not loosen" sentences still in the skill, and fix the #63/#49 citations.

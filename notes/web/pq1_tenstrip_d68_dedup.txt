Hey. 

 Assuming you’re talking about my workflow (it’s not something “official” by TenStrip), the continuation feature always requires AND produces 24fps video. 

 I’ve found some instances in which beta6 generates videos that “seems” to be lower fps, but almost always that can be counteracted either with a better seed, with a appropriate prompt adjustment, disabling a conflicting Lora, or playing with the sampler/scheduler settings. VSA sparse attn node can also have something to do with that. 

 First I would do is rewording your prompt, and adding something in the line of “dynamic video, fluid natural motions” with different seeds. If that doesn’t work, maybe changing the scheduler settings to res_multistep or even lcm or euler. Finally, bypassing the sparse attention node can be used too for troubleshooting but I don’t think that’s the cause. 

 Besides the guiding and conditioning, nothing is done differently for a continuation video than for a straight single normal one. So if the first part looks good and the second one have that slow mo feeling, it’s something particular to that second prompt or scene
